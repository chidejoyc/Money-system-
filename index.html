About the author

Meet Coach Clinton.

I'm Coach Clinton — and before I ever wrote a word of this book, I was the person struggling with the exact problem it solves. I worked as a freelancer, later became a salary earner too, and no matter how much came in, my money never lasted more than two weeks. I spent rapidly, without direction, and had nothing to show for it at the end of the month.

I created The Money Master System after doing deep research into how to actually manage money — not just earn it. I learned to spend on purpose instead of on impulse, and to build a real system instead of relying on willpower alone. I'm still on this journey myself, and I'm not pretending to have it all figured out. But the system in this book has already helped a few people reshape how they handle their money — and I built it so more people don't have to learn the hard way, the way I did.
